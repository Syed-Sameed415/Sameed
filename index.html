Background

My path through engineering has taken me across circuit design, MEMS fabrication, control systems, soft robotics, and most recently, a capstone project building a portable milk heating and frothing device for a real Kelowna coffee company.

I like problems that don't have one obvious answer. The work I find most rewarding involves making something tangible, testing it, watching it fail, and figuring out why. That iteration loop of build, test, learn, repeat is what draws me to towards more hands on enginering.

Other than the technical side, I care about clear communication and working well in teams where people bring genuinely different perspectives. My background also includes certifications in industrial PLCs and robotics, which has given me exposure to automation and manufacturing environments beyond the university lab.
